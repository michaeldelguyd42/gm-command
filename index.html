1 Pick a vertical

Top: Both shows everything. Filter hides Lighting. Lighting hides Filter. Pick whichever you're working on this minute.

2 Open "Now / Today"

The clock at top tells you which OODA phase you're in (Observe / Orient / Decide / Act). The big colored card tells you what to do right now. Just do that.

3 Tap the Checklist

Tap a box when you finish. The bar at the bottom of the screen shows how much of your day is done. Goal: green bar full by 9 PM.

4 Update KPIs once a day

Open the KPIs tab. Type today's numbers. Red = behind. Green = on track. Source numbers from dashboard.noclogs.com.

5 Roster - confirm reps

In Reps, tap a slot box to cycle: blank -> Confirmed -> Shaky -> x No-go. Do this twice a day.

6 Before a tough sit - Battlecard

Open the Battlecard tab on your phone. Read the competitor's strengths, weaknesses, and your counter. Two minutes of prep beats five hours of guesswork.

7 End of day: Issue tomorrow's leads by 9:01 PM

This is the hard deadline. Then hit Reset Day on the Now/Today tab. Wake up tomorrow with a clean checklist.

Important: your data is per-device

Each device (laptop, phone) has its own copy of your KPIs, checklist progress, and roster. They do not sync automatically. That's intentional - keeps everything private. Use the Export button on the top bar if you ever want to copy a day's data between devices.

The Daily OODA Loop Observe -> Orient -> Decide -> Act

A fighter-pilot decision cycle. Whoever loops fastest wins. Run it once per day, with mini-loops inside each block. Same loop for Filter and Lighting - you're running one day.

6:00-9:00 AM

OBSERVE

What happened yesterday & overnight?

  • KPI dashboard - yesterday's leads / sits / sales / $ (both)
  • SDHL: next-day coverage, Filter then Lighting
  • Email + Teams + GroupMe scan for fires
  • IJNF / hold / install / retail spreadsheets
  • Escalated services queue
9:00 AM-12:00 PM

ORIENT

Where are the gaps and who needs what?

  • Submit leads by 8 AM
  • Check today's lead sheet for dups
  • Rep cal - confirmed vs shaky (both verticals)
  • PM meeting - installs / hold / retail / IJNF
  • Pick the 1 rep who needs you today (alternate Filter / Lighting weekly)
12:00-3:00 PM

DECIDE

What's the priority play?

  • Coaching priority: who, what, when
  • Rehash: 5 Filter + 3 Lighting
  • Recruiting: 3 outreach moves on Fountain
  • Tonight's huddle training topic
  • Hold spreadsheet decisions (both)
3:00-9:01 PM

ACT

Execute and prep tomorrow.

  • Run leads / phone drops (Lighting dusk demos prioritized)
  • Scrub each sale's PPWK
  • Manage lead sheet
  • Issue next-day leads by 9:01 PM (both)
  • Confirm rep confirmations

Why OODA works for a two-vertical GM

Filter is volume with a tight close. Lighting is fewer leads, higher ticket, longer sit. Different cadence, same brain. Without OODA you'll spend the day chasing whichever fire is loudest. With it: look at both verticals, orient on where the gap actually is, decide the one move that moves the needle, act. The trap is jumping straight to ACT in one vertical while the other slips quietly.

      CLE FILTER Cleveland gutter-protection

      Universal Filter counter-objections

      • "Cheaper option online": compare warranty - most are not transferable, ours is. Labor warranty is what costs.
      • "Already have guards": ride-along; most "guards" are screens - different category. Show micromesh demo.
      • "Need to think": appointment-day pricing tied to install schedule. Be specific, not pushy.
      • "It's just leaves": show ice-dam, mosquito-breeding, foundation-erosion photos. Gutters protect the house, not just look pretty.

      CLE LIGHTING Cleveland permanent outdoor lighting

      Universal Lighting counter-objections

      • "I already do Christmas lights": our system is permanent + everyday accent + holidays + party scenes from the app. Pays back in ~3 seasons vs annual rental.
      • "Too expensive": compare the 10-year cost: annual seasonal rental is ~$1.5-3K/year on a 2-story. Permanent is once. Plus daily curb appeal value.
      • "Looks tacky in daylight": aluminum channel is recessed, color-matched. Show the daytime fascia shot vs. the dusk activation video.
      • "I'll do it myself with Home Depot lights": bulb density, channel rigidity, app integration, install warranty. Side-by-side fixture demo.
      • "Need to think": install windows fill fast in Q3/Q4. Lock the deposit, lock the slot. Same pricing logic as Filter - tied to schedule.
